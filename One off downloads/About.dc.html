About

I make complex things understandable.

I have spent my career helping people see complicated things clearly enough to do something about them.

Sometimes that means interviewing customers in the field. Sometimes it means aligning executives around a service model, untangling a product ecosystem, building a design team, or making the prototype myself. The medium changes. The job is still understanding.

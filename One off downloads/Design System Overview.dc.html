I’m Bruce. Home Design system Sections Available

The system, on one page.

One ink, one accent, a short ladder of warm papers, and Standerd throughout. Every page is a two-column grid: a numbered rail, then the content. Rules are hairlines. Corners are square. The background moves and the text does not.

01 Color

One ink, one accent, four greys of paper.

The accent is reserved for section markers, numerals, arrows and links. It is never a fill behind text. Green exists only as the availability dot. No gradients anywhere.

02 Type

Standerd, four weights, tight display tracking.

Display sizes are fluid clamp pairs with negative tracking. Body holds at 16px and 1.65. Measures are capped so a line never runs past comfortable reading: 26em on headings, 34em on body, 36em on lead.

05 Elevation

One shadow, or none.

A single three-layer shadow carries anything that should read as a physical card. Bento grids, featured blocks and artifact images hold it permanently; evidence tiles and deliverable cards earn it on hover. Rules, rows, tags, panels and the nav never have it.

06 Interaction

Background moves. Text does not.

Hovering a clickable block tints it and nudges its arrow six pixels. Titles hold at ink. Images zoom on the inner element only. Press states are not styled, and there are no entrance animations.

08 Voice

It names the number, not the feeling.

→First person for the author, “we” for the engagement team. Never “you.”
→Sentence case everywhere — headings, labels, nav, buttons.
→Headings are statements with a period. Labels are plain nouns.
→Section markers are two digits and a noun: 01 Color, 04 Decisions.
→No emoji, no exclamation marks, no rhetorical questions.
Case studies always run in the same order: context, objectives, my role, approach, decisions, outcomes, results, recognition, learnings, deliverables, contact. Sections are omitted rather than renamed.
